# About this handbook

The intended reader has hands-on experience with AWS, Kubernetes, CI/CD, IaC, and observability and wants sharper senior-level judgment. The content emphasizes assumptions, failure domains, and operational ownership rather than tool trivia.

## Boundaries

All examples are sanitized and illustrative. Never copy internal topology, logs, customer data, credentials, account identifiers, or incident timelines into a public repository. Confirm provider behavior against linked official documentation before a production change.

## Editorial model

Pages distinguish concepts, executable exercises, troubleshooting evidence, and interview reasoning. A command is useful only when its scope, expected signal, and risk are clear.
