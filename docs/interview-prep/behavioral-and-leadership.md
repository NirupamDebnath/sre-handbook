# Behavioral and leadership interview bank

Use STAR (situation, task, action, result), but add what you learned and how you measured the result. Keep examples sanitized.

1. **Led an incident:** explain command roles, impact updates, mitigation choice, and learning actions.
2. **Disagreed on reliability investment:** quantify risk and alternatives; seek a reversible decision.
3. **Reduced toil:** baseline effort/error rate, automate with guardrails, and measure durable benefit.
4. **Influenced without authority:** learn incentives, offer a pilot, publish evidence, and co-own adoption.
5. **Made a mistake:** own it, mitigate, communicate, and show the control that prevents recurrence.
6. **Prioritized competing incidents:** use user impact, safety, urgency, owners, and transparent communication.
7. **Improved an on-call program:** measure page quality and load, remove non-actionable alerts, rehearse escalation.
8. **Mentored an engineer:** set a scoped outcome, pair on reasoning, give feedback, and show independent growth.
9. **Handled ambiguous requirements:** clarify users/SLOs/constraints, state assumptions, deliver a small validated increment.
10. **Communicated risk to leadership:** lead with impact and decision needed, present options/costs, avoid false certainty.
